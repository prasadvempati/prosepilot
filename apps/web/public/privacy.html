Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Your writing stays yours. ProsePilot never uses your text to train AI models. Your documents are processed in real-time and discarded immediately.

What We Collect

ProsePilot collects minimal data to provide the service:

  • Account information: Email address and name (via Clerk authentication)
  • Voice profile: Writing style analysis stored locally on our servers, associated with your account
  • Usage metadata: Number of checks performed, features used (not the content of your writing)

What We Never Do

  • We never store the text you submit for grammar checking
  • We never use your writing to train AI models
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • We never read or access your documents outside of real-time processing

How Grammar Checking Works

When you submit text for checking, it is processed in real-time by our AI engine. The text is sent over an encrypted connection, analyzed, and the results are returned to you. The original text is not stored after processing is complete.

Voice Profiles

Voice profiles analyze your writing style (sentence length, vocabulary, punctuation habits) to personalize grammar suggestions. The profile stores statistical patterns, not your actual text. You can delete your voice profile at any time from the app settings.

Word Document Processing

When you upload a .docx file, it is processed entirely on our server. The file and its contents are deleted immediately after processing. We do not retain copies of uploaded documents.

Browser Extension

The ProsePilot browser extension checks text you select on web pages. Text is sent to our API for processing only when you explicitly click "Check Selection." The extension does not read or transmit any other page content.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over TLS-encrypted connections. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@prosepilot.io.
